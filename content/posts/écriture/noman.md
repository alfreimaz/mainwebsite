### no man's land is another man's treasure

you asked me to meet you halfway <br/>
and so i did<br/>
i came one day running to you<br/>
while you remained seated<br/>
said that you had already walked<br/>
run<br/>
climbed<br/>
and nearly drowned<br/>
carrying a chair to eventually rest<br/>
and a table on your back<br/>
all so that we could talk<br/>
talk facts<br/>
and form<br/>
away from the expectations<br/>
away from what we should have said<br/>
but didn't want<br/>
and yet you rarely talked yourself<br/>
except to say<br/>
how right i was<br/>
how strong i was<br/>
worthy<br/>
unique<br/>
precious<br/>
or endangered<br/>
never from what<br/>
never from when<br/>
often from where<br/>
too often for my taste<br/>
but still at the time i would nod<br/>
at first just so that you would stop<br/>
soon enough out of pure habit<br/>
and i would listen to your praise<br/>
and sleep on the floor<br/>
and dream on the floor<br/>
for i didn't bring my own chair<br/>
and for i cared not to go back<br/>
i traded comfort for the truth<br/>
and found a truth made for comfort<br/>
i never slept as good as then<br/>
knowing how right we were<br/>
but one day i woke up to sounds<br/>
sounds of iron scraping the stones<br/>
denting the common ground<br/>
you had pushed the table<br/>
a meter further<br/>
closer to my hometown<br/>
you said that once again<br/>
the halfway point had moved<br/>
and i for once wanted to check<br/>
thus went to see with my own eyes<br/>
the extreme trenches you had left<br/>
a table on your back<br/>
and a mission in your head<br/>
but as soon as i took a step<br/>
i nearly fell into the void<br/>
there was nothing there on the other side<br/>
the halfway mark<br/>
was but on the end of the line
