### Rear view belvedere

Beauty lingers longer than the remnants<br/>
It haunts the eye and breath of those who rest decayed<br/>
Their bones a puzzle<br/>
Their minds a veil<br/>
And yet we still look back to look the same.

Our comparative forms supplanting all<br/>
The sounds of rattled errs in brittle throats<br/>
Hoping through gouging eyes<br/>
To throw a glance behind<br/>
Where better lies.

Caretakers of a landscape<br/>
With eye floaters for clouds<br/>
Where grass is always greener<br/>
At least the green you know.

There is no greater fear<br/>
Than, in the change of a portrait,<br/>
To lose the one you loved.<br/>

I’ll stick to what I’ve always done.

### Progress is never joy

Like a dog outrunning its tail<br/>
Convincing itself<br/>
The bite doesn’t hurt.

### Eat the Sun

For some sunflowers strive at night,<br/>
In stargazing.
